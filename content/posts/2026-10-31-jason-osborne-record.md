Jason Osborne is the Majority Leader of the New Hampshire House and is running
to be its Speaker after Tuesday's election. This page is the public record on
him, in two parts: what he has done in office, and what has been reported about
him. There are no adjectives in it on purpose. Follow the links and check.

## Who he is

- From Defiance, Ohio, where his family founded Credit Adjustments, Inc., a debt
collection company, in 1964. He joined the firm in 1995 and worked as its CIO and
later CEO.
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Osborne_(politician)), with cites to
the company's own site)
- Moved to New Hampshire in 2010 as part of the Free State Project.
([Concord Monitor](https://www.concordmonitor.com/Granite-State-News-Collaborative-42176207),
[Washington Monthly](https://washingtonmonthly.com/2021/11/07/the-white-mountain-boys/))
- Elected to the House in 2014. Represents Rockingham 2: Auburn, Chester, and Sandown.
([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Jason_Osborne_(New_Hampshire)))
- Chosen House Majority Leader by the Republican caucus in November 2020; has held the
job since.
([InsideSources](https://www.insidesources.com/jason-osborne-will-be-new-house-majority-leader/))
- In June 2026 he announced he is running for Speaker for the term that begins after
the November 2026 election. He is one of several Republican candidates.
([NHPR, June 23, 2026](https://www.nhpr.org/politics/2026-06-23/gop-leader-in-the-nh-house-announces-run-for-speaker-jason-osborne),
[InDepthNH, June 25, 2026](https://indepthnh.org/2026/06/25/osborne-has-history-in-nh-house-as-he-runs-for-speaker/))

## What he has done in office

This is the record he runs on. In announcing for Speaker he summarized six years
of Republican majorities as cutting taxes, expanding school choice and parental
rights, protecting gun rights, and rejecting an income tax.
([InDepthNH](https://indepthnh.org/2026/06/25/osborne-has-history-in-nh-house-as-he-runs-for-speaker/))

- **Education Freedom Accounts.** Called HB 20, the bill that created the EFA
voucher program, the majority's "number one priority" in 2021. Voted to keep and
expand the program every year since (HB 1683 in 2022; HB 367 and HB 464 in 2023;
HB 1561, HB 1634, HB 1665, HB 1677 in 2024), and against HB 1512, which would have
capped it to a budget. In 2025 the program was made universal (SB 295, signed
June 10, 2025).
([NH House GOP press release](https://www.nhhousegop.com/?p=8253),
[Citizens Count voting record](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/jason-jessie-osborne/running),
[SB 295 bill status](https://gc.nh.gov/bill_Status/billinfo.aspx?id=965))
- **Parental bill of rights.** HB 10, signed June 10, 2025, effective July 1, 2025.
([HB 10 bill status](https://gc.nh.gov/bill_Status/billinfo.aspx?id=49))
- **State investment in digital assets.** HB 302 (2025) lets the state treasury invest
in precious metals and digital assets; New Hampshire was the first state to do so.
([Wikipedia, citing session laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Osborne_(politician)))
- **Abortion.** Voted for SB 66 (2017), which allows murder charges for causing the
death of a fetus; for HB 625 (2021), the 24-week abortion ban; to repeal the
buffer-zone law; and against requiring maternity-coverage plans to cover abortion.
In 2022 he voted with Democrats to table HB 1477, a fetal-heartbeat ban.
([VoteSmart](https://justfacts.votesmart.org/candidate/key-votes/154541/jessie-osborne/2/abortion),
[FastDemocracy, HB 1477](https://fastdemocracy.com/bill-search/nh/2022/bills/NHB00007982/))
- **Guns.** In June 2022 he proposed yearly firearms training in public schools at
every grade level, and called federal gun-safety proposals "fruity ideas."
([Tweet, June 12, 2022](https://twitter.com/Osborne4NH/status/1535781796310405122),
[WMUR CloseUp](https://www.wmur.com/article/closeup-nh-house-majority-leader-promises-no-new-abortion-restrictions/40264783))
- **Cannabis.** Wrote a 2023 op-ed making the conservative case for legalization.
([Union Leader](https://www.unionleader.com/opinion/op-eds/rep-jason-osborne-conservative-case-for-cannabis-reform/article_c1b60b00-868b-5903-83ce-9c99a0ed0758.html))
- **Right to work.** Backed legislation barring union-membership requirements in
collective bargaining agreements.
([NH House GOP](https://www.nhhousegop.com/author/admin/page/22/))
- Endorsed Ron DeSantis for president in 2023.
- Co-sponsored 31 bills enacted into law in the 2025 and 2026 sessions.
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Osborne_(politician)))

Supporters credit him with winning conservative votes on a slim majority by
keeping his caucus unified; NHPR profiled that style in 2021.
([NHPR, June 14, 2021](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2021-06-14/out-of-public-eye-jason-osborne-helps-lead-historic-push-by-gop-in-n-h-house))

## What has been reported about him

### Mammoth Tech (2022)

Reported by Bob Sanders for the NH Business Review and republished by NHPR on
July 13, 2022.
([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2022-07-13/inside-the-decline-of-the-nh-house-majority-leader-jason-osborne-mammoth-tech-inc),
[NHBR](https://www.nhbr.com/the-decline-and-fall-of-new-hampshire-house-majority-leaders-mammoth-tech-inc/))

- His family company, Credit Adjustments Inc., renamed Mammoth Tech in 2021, received
about $4 million in Paycheck Protection Program loans in April 2020, money intended
to keep employees on payroll.
([ProPublica PPP tracker](https://projects.propublica.org/coronavirus/bailouts/loans/credit-adjustments-inc-9632847107))
- On March 2, 2022, the company fired its remaining New Hampshire employees, and
roughly 500 others in Ohio and elsewhere, by mass email, without advance notice.
Osborne's name was at the bottom of the email. His 2020 campaign website had said he
brought over 100 good jobs to the community.
- The company then faced a federal class action in Ohio under the WARN Act for
failing to give 60 days' notice; a suit from its Manchester landlord over unpaid
rent; a roughly $1 million judgment to a staffing contractor and a $181,000 judgment
to a management firm; and two disability-discrimination suits from former pregnant
employees, one settled and one pending at the time of the report. As of early 2023
the class action was still active.
- His stated reason, in the layoff email, was that giving notice might have hurt the
company's effort to find capital. A former manager told the paper that executives
had let slip in late February that the company would close in mid-March.
- He did not return the reporter's calls. His father, the company's founder and CEO,
said he had been asked by his son not to talk to the press.

### Old forum posts (2022)

Reported by Ethan DeWitt for the New Hampshire Bulletin, republished by NHPR on
September 1, 2022.
([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2022-09-01/n-h-house-majority-leader-used-racist-slur-on-online-forum-resurfaced-post-shows),
[NH Bulletin](https://newhampshirebulletin.com/2022/09/01/nh-house-majority-leader-used-racial-slur-on-online-forum-resurfaced-post-shows/),
[WMUR](https://www.wmur.com/article/nh-house-majority-leader-regret-racial-slur/41059110))

- Between 2006 and 2015 he wrote 1,241 posts on the forum for the libertarian radio
show Free Talk Live. The Bulletin archived all of them before the site went offline.
- In an April 2011 post, when he was 33, he used a racial slur for Black people three
times, in a hypothetical about whether to associate with someone who supported
lynching. In a 2010 post he described berating a woman at a grocery store over
breastfeeding. In a 2007 post he questioned whether the law should set an age of
consent at all and suggested letting the market decide case by case.
- His response: he said he did not recall writing the slur post, that it contained
language he would not use today, and that its intent was to condemn racism and
pedophilia. He said he has changed since: stopped drinking, lost weight, returned to
church. He now says an age of consent should be upheld in law. Gov. Sununu called
the words inappropriate and wrong regardless of context.
- In January 2021, when a Democratic representative used the same slur, Osborne had
issued a press release saying her comments had no place in society.

### Campaign finance (2025)

- On October 17, 2025, the Attorney General's election law unit issued cease-and-desist
orders and fines to several State House figures. Osborne and his committee, Friends
of Jason Osborne, were fined $2,000 for filing required finance reports more than 33
months late for the 2022 cycle and more than 10 months late for the 2024 cycle. The
AG's office had first asked him for the missing reports in a letter dated January 24,
2025.
([NHPR, Oct. 17, 2025](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2025-10-17/top-state-house-officials-cited-for-campaign-finance-violations),
[NH Bulletin, Oct. 20, 2025](https://newhampshirebulletin.com/2025/10/20/republican-lawmakers-democratic-executive-councilor-fined-for-campaign-finance-violations/),
[Patch](https://patch.com/new-hampshire/concord-nh/4-gop-house-leaders-gop-house-pac-democratic-executive-councilor-named))
- For the record, the same round of enforcement also cited the Speaker, the Deputy
Majority Leader, the House GOP PAC, and the Executive Council's lone Democrat.

### Education Freedom Accounts and his family (2026)

- Other lawmakers have questioned his advocacy for the EFA program while his wife's
organization receives money from it. Latitude Learning Resources, where Sharon
Osborne is chair and director, received $15,900 in EFA funds for the 2024–25 school
year, according to the Children's Scholarship Fund NH's expenditure reports. His
children do not attend public school.
([InDepthNH, June 25, 2026](https://indepthnh.org/2026/06/25/osborne-has-history-in-nh-house-as-he-runs-for-speaker/),
[WMUR CloseUp, 2022](https://www.wmur.com/article/closeup-nh-house-majority-leader-promises-no-new-abortion-restrictions/40264783))

## In his own defense

He has answered most of this on the record. On the forum posts, that he was a
different person then and that his colleagues know where he came from. On the
layoffs, that notice would have jeopardized a rescue. On the campaign reports, the
fine was paid under a cease-and-desist order, and the same day's orders went to
leaders of both parties. On the EFA program, he has said from the first draft the
intent was for it to be universal. His supporters would add that he has delivered
the votes he promised on a thin majority for six years, which is the job of a
majority leader. The links above carry his side as well as the reporting; read both.
