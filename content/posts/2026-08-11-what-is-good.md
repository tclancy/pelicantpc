## Good in the last month

- Step bits: how did I not know about these? Bought a small set to put holes in a metal
planter and they worked perfectly, then the smaller ones came in handy when I needed
to make holes in some plastic electronic project cases I'd bought that refused to pop
out parts where the parts were meant to pop out. Wound up making a hole just big enough
to get the USB-C end inside the box
- Charlotte Cornfield's new album. It's half hit and half miss for me, but oh the hits
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RlYNPkFyC1w
- _[Wake in Fright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wake_in_Fright)_. Allow me to lead off
by strongly suggesting you _do not_ watch this, but it was an amazing ride. One of the
best "horror movie that's actually not horror, just reality through a different lens".
All that said, trigger warnings ahoy as it features a literal **real** kangaroo hunt. Never
scrubbing that from my head. Plus side, it led to the end of the practice.
- Buying a used Thule and rehabbing it. My wife got one for camping and skiing a few years
back and it was a giant pain to work with. Last year I decided I was annoyed enough to
take apart the clamp pieces, wire brush them and paint them, rebuild the bumper bits
with two-sided cushioned tape, swap out the old hardware for new and 3-in-1 oil every
last metal bit I could find. And it was better. Fast-forward to camping last week and
it might be even easier to use than last year.
- Camping at White Lake in Tamorth, New Hampshire: it is always SWASS-alert levels of
hot in early August but the beach looking out at the lake sitting in front of a number
of White Mountains is a "Wish You Were Here!" postcard type of view. And it's just a
damned friendly place.
- _Exile on Main Street_: not exactly news but what is called the [Deluxe Version on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/1D0PTM0bg7skufClSUOxTP?si=CQR0ClfRTPy7lZWeFEJt2g) has a whole second album of
songs that didn't make the cut and they are fantastic. Like some of the songs describe other,
famous, band's whole oeuvre.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hJx4xwjTb6M
